# Audio credits

No audio files are used right now. Every sound (coin chime, beanbag boing, book/can thud,
win jingle) and the background music loop are generated live with Web Audio in
`src/audio/sfx.ts`.

The owner's own MP3s will replace them: add each clip here (source file, what it's used for,
how it was trimmed) and list it in `AUDIO_FILES` in `src/audio/sfx.ts`.

(An earlier build used Adobe "Aero Audio Starter Assets" clips; they were removed at the
owner's request.)
